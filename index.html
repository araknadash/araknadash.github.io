About

I'm a Sr. Finance Associate at Fidelity Investments, working at the intersection of investment data, research insight, and emerging AI capabilities — building on a couple of years managing data analytics & insights for a portfolio platform, and, before that, on graduate research in reinforcement learning.

I moved into finance because I wanted my work to touch the decisions that actually shape people's lives — how they save, invest, and plan for the future they want. What keeps me there is that I'm genuinely obsessed with problems: hand me a messy workflow, an unexplained return series, or a dashboard nobody trusts, and I can't let it go until I understand why it's built that way and whether it could work better. That's the same itch that had me decoding neural signals to control a robot arm in grad school and building emergency-brake electronics for a Hyperloop pod — different domains, same compulsion to take the system apart and rebuild it cleaner.

Right now that means translating dense data into investment narratives PMs and analysts can actually use, and strengthening the finance side of that equation through CFA Foundations and AI-in-finance coursework. When I'm off the clock, I'm usually exploring prompting techniques for LLMs, hiking, or shipping small personal web apps.
